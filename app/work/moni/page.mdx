<CaseSection n="05" title="Architecture">
  The app follows a feature-sliced layout with a deliberate Domain/Persistence
  separation. The content view holds live @Query arrays and exposes computed
  values like activeAccounts and totals; the Domain layer is stateless enums of
  pure functions (balance math, SMS parsing, formatting); and FinanceStore is
  the single boundary that AppIntents use to read and write. Keeping the parser
  and calculator pure made them trivially testable without any UI on the
  stack.
</CaseSection>
